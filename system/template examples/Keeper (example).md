# Everyday coffee beans (example)

> [!example] Example only. A filled-in keeper showing how the template is used; not a real finding.

## The finding

A medium roast from a local roaster, bought whole and used within 4 weeks of the roast date, beat every supermarket brand tried.

## Why this one

- Freshness mattered more than origin or brand.
- Medium roast worked for both espresso and filter.

## How to use it

Buy 250 g at a time; grind just before brewing.
