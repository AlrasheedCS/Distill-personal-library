# Explore a new topic from zero

> [!info] Starter prompt shipped with Distill. Edit it, rename it or delete it.

## When to use it

A map of a field before you dive in.

## Prompt

```text
I know nothing about {topic}. Give me a map:
- The 5 core ideas, each in two sentences.
- Key terms I'll keep seeing, with plain definitions.
- The main schools of thought or debates.
- 3 beginner and 3 advanced resources (books, papers, courses), with links.
- 5 good questions to ask next.
```

- `{topic}`
