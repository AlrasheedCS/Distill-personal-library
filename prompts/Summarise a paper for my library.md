# Summarise a paper for my library

> [!info] Starter prompt shipped with Distill. Edit it, rename it or delete it.

## When to use it

Turn one paper into a Paper note.

## Prompt

```text
Read {paper} and fill in my Paper note:
- Main finding: one sentence.
- Method in brief: what they did, with whom or on what, how big.
- Limits: what it can't tell us.
- 3–5 key results, each with page or section, labelled [source].
- Anything you conclude yourself, labelled [inferred].
Quote numbers exactly; don't round or reinterpret them.
```

## Variables

- `{paper (link}`
- `{PDF or pasted text)}`
