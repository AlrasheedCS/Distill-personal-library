# Explore a food or cuisine

> [!info] Starter prompt shipped with Distill. Edit it, rename it or delete it.

## When to use it

Get to know a dish, ingredient or cuisine quickly.

## Prompt

```text
I want to explore {topic}. Give me:
1. What it is and where it comes from, in 3 sentences.
2. The 5 dishes or uses most worth trying first, each with why.
3. Key ingredients and techniques, and common beginner mistakes.
4. One easy recipe to start with, with quantities.
5. Where to learn more: 3 trustworthy sources (books, sites, people), with links.
Mark anything you're unsure of.
```
